Check Report

Use this tool to check whether the IRS has flagged a transaction report associated with your name. If a report was flagged, you can review the details and take the next steps to resolve it.

Check your flagged transaction report

Enter the information below exactly as it appears on your tax records. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

Enter your full legal name as it appears on your most recent tax return.

Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (example: 05/20/1990).

Enter only the last four digits of your Social Security number.

Leave blank to check all available years.

Checking your report...

Note: For security, this page checks flagged transaction reports using only the information you provide. The IRS never sends unsolicited emails or text messages requesting this information.

What is a flagged transaction report?

In certain situations, financial institutions and businesses report transactions to the IRS that may require additional review. When a report is flagged, it means additional information may be needed to confirm that the activity is consistent with your tax records.

Flagged transaction reports may relate to activity such as large cash deposits or withdrawals, wire transfers, or other reportable transactions. Not all flagged reports indicate a problem — many are resolved automatically after review.

What you'll need

  • Your full legal name as it appears on your tax return
  • Your date of birth
  • The last 4 digits of your Social Security number
  • Your tax year (optional)

1. Enter your information

Provide your full legal name, date of birth, and the last 4 digits of your Social Security number.

2. Review your results

We'll show whether any flagged transaction reports match the information you provided.

3. Take the next step

If a report is flagged, follow the instructions provided or contact the IRS for assistance.

Privacy and security

Your information is protected and used only to verify your identity and check your report status. The IRS uses industry-standard encryption to safeguard the information you provide. Review the IRS Privacy Policy for more information.

Need help?

  • If you believe you've been a victim of identity theft, visit Identity Theft Central.
  • If you need to report suspicious activity, visit Report Fraud.
  • For other questions, visit Let Us Help You.
